# 🌅 Vodou Morning Brief

---

**You just activated your personal intelligence analyst.**

Vodou Morning Brief runs autonomously every morning to gather intelligence from your system, memories, and available data sources — delivering a personalized daily brief **privately, locally, and automatically**.

---

## 🎯 What This Skill Does

**Morning Brief provides:**
1. **System Health Check** - CPU, memory, disk status
2. **Memory Insights** - Recent important thoughts and patterns from your memory system
3. **Daily Focus** - Recommendations based on your patterns
4. **Optional Modules** - Calendar, weather, custom data sources (expandable)

**All running locally. All private. All yours.**

---

## ⚙️ Setup Your Morning Brief

**What you're choosing:** How to configure and run your morning brief.

1. **Generate Brief Now** - Run it immediately to see what it looks like
2. **Configure Schedule** - Set up automatic daily delivery (requires cron/launchd setup)
3. **Customize Sources** - Choose what data to include in your brief
4. **View Example** - See a sample brief first
5. **Advanced Setup** - Full automation with notifications

**Reply with the number (1-5).**

---

## 📊 Default Brief Includes

- ⚙️ **System Status**: CPU, memory, disk health
- 🧠 **Memory Insights**: Recent learnings from Vodou memory
- 📅 **Date & Time**: Current day and schedule awareness
- 💡 **Focus Suggestion**: AI-generated daily priority
