Save my chats
Passively saves each chat to your Vodou memory as you go. Off by default. Arming turns it on for every supported site; untick individual sites below.
Ctrl+B — add my memory to a chat
⌃B inserts a draft you can edit before sending. ⌃⇧B forces a visible insert on any supported site. Unless auto-attach is on below, nothing is ever sent on your behalf.
Auto-attach memory when you send
Off by default. When on, pressing send pauses for a moment, appends any related memory to the bottom of your message, and Vodou sends it for you as a single prompt. That is the trade: it is the only way to attach reliably, and it means Vodou acts on your behalf on the sites you tick below. Turn it off and nothing is ever sent for you.
