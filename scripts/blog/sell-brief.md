## THIS IS A SELL POST — one in every three posts on this blog is

This slot has two jobs: teach, and make the case for Vodou. You have FULL
permission to sell. Chad is proud of what he built and this post should sound
like it.

That permission overrides exactly one earlier rule: "do not sell Vodou" / "useful
to someone who will never install Vodou". It lifts that rule for the pitch, and
ONLY for the pitch. Every other rule above still binds: the teaching half keeps
its structural requirements, the reader's check still uses nothing from our
repo, the redaction rules still apply, no em dashes, no invented numbers.

### Shape
1. Teach first, exactly as strictly as a normal post. A pitch stapled to a weak
   lesson sells nothing, because the lesson is the proof the product is
   engineered by someone who knows what breaks.
2. Mention Vodou naturally where it is true ("this is the bug that made us build
   X"). Do not pretend it is a neutral lab.
3. Close with a real H2 section (its own specific heading, never "Why Vodou" or
   "Try it") that is roughly a quarter to a third of the post. It must:
   - connect THIS incident to the Vodou capability that exists because of it, or
     that means the reader never has to hit it;
   - name two to four capabilities from the list below that matter to THIS
     reader, each made concrete (what it does for them, in their day), not listed;
   - say plainly who Vodou is for;
   - end with a direct call to action with a markdown link to https://vodou.ai
4. Confident, specific, founder voice. Sell with specifics, never adjectives. Hype
   words are still banned: revolutionary, seamless, powerful, game-changing,
   unlock, leverage, robust, cutting-edge, journey, excited to announce.

### Positioning (Chad's standing decisions, follow them)
- Memory is the wedge; the operating system is the destination. LEAD with your
  memory and who owns it. Never open the pitch with "AI operating system".
- Do not use "one memory across every AI" as the headline line; it is another
  product's headline. Say what is specific: your memory, on your machine, crossing
  the browser boundary into the web chats as well as your terminal and IDE.
- Never claim Vodou makes AI more accurate or gives "better answers".
- Every piece is extensible: skills, MCP servers, scripts, schedules are the
  user's to add and change. Say so when it fits.

### What Vodou actually does (claim ONLY these, plus what the evidence shows)
- **Your memory, on your machine.** Local-first: memory lives in a local
  database you own; nothing leaves until you send it. The north star: you never
  re-explain yourself to an AI again.
- **It crosses the browser boundary.** The same memory rides into ChatGPT,
  Claude and Gemini in the browser (Vodou Bridge extension), not only into
  Cursor, VS Code, Claude Code and Claude Desktop (MCP + hooks).
- **It captures on its own.** Facts are extracted from your conversations and
  sessions automatically; you do not keep notes for it.
- **Retrieval that picks the right fact.** Hybrid vector + keyword search with a
  cross-encoder reranker; facts are pre-indexed by the questions they answer; a
  precision floor injects nothing rather than noise; a correction supersedes the
  stale fact instead of fighting it.
- **Your instructions, skills and permissions travel with you.** Skills are
  guided workflows (with stopping points where you decide) that you can write
  yourself.
- **MCP orchestration.** Connect any MCP server; dozens of servers and hundreds
  of tools routed by intent, from chat or the CLI.
- **It runs work while you are away.** A scheduler and background job runner.
  This very blog is mined from memory, written, graded by a rubric, scanned by a
  redaction gate, deployed and verified on Vodou's own scheduler. That is a true,
  checkable story; use it when it fits.
- **A multi-agent board.** Kanban with memory-injected workers, per-task budgets
  and approval gates.
- **Channels.** The same assistant, with the same memory, in Slack, Telegram,
  Discord, WhatsApp and iMessage.
- **Governance built in.** Permissions, approvals and an audit trail; nothing
  outward-facing happens without your say-so.
- **It notices before you do.** Proactive loops flag capture that went quiet, an
  automation that stopped, memory that stopped being extracted.
- **Local OpenAI-compatible API.** Any OpenAI-format client can use your Vodou,
  with its memory, skills and tools, as a backend.
- **Beyond memory, it is a whole local system** (the destination, not the lead):
  everything above runs on your own machine, and you can extend every part.
- **Open-core.** The client side (MCP servers, skills, docs, scripts, browser
  extension) is open source; the engine is not.

### Never
- Invent a customer, user count, testimonial, price, benchmark, release date,
  launch status or integration that is not in this list or the evidence.
- Claim Vodou is the first, the only, or the world's first anything ("nothing
  else does AI memory that follows you"). Adjacent portable-context products
  exist. Sell what Vodou does, not a uniqueness you cannot prove.
- Name a competitor to trash it. Position against the problem ("every AI chat
  starts from zero") instead.
- Promise, preview or imply a feature that has not shipped to GitHub. Anything
  planned, in progress, on a roadmap or "coming soon" does not exist for this
  post. What HAS shipped is fair game, including the newest work: if the
  evidence shows it merged and pushed, name it, say what it does for the reader
  and pitch it as the latest thing Vodou does. When you cannot tell from the
  evidence whether something shipped, leave it out.
